# Product decisions

- The default experience is real private guest use, not a pre-populated demo. There are no fabricated emotions, weather, correlations or social proof.
- Mood + energy are immediate; four more dimensions are progressively disclosed. No question asks for the reason. Notes never improve a score or unlock a feature.
- A local deterministic reflection is always available. Optional AI is authenticated, metered, consented and reviewed before saving. AI provider/model selection is deployment configuration rather than an unverified vendor recommendation.
- Text retention is off by default. Strong user-held encryption is preferable to silently storing a key beside the encrypted note. This creates a real recovery tradeoff explained before saving.
- User corrections are authoritative and auditable. Original model values are not silently rewritten; current snapshots and revision history are separate.
- Editorial serif typography, charcoal/olive surfaces and a locally generated lunar sphere avoid stock imagery, remote font tracking and generic dashboard styling. The illustration is atmospheric rather than a scientifically accurate map of lunar terrain; lighting follows phase, with a fixed northern-style orientation.
- The Moon is one explanatory variable. Weekday and self-reported restfulness compete under the same evidence rules. Differences are response-scale points, not a health score or causal inference.
- Weekly and monthly summaries are descriptive and available immediately; no arbitrary paywall or 90-day wait. Lunar comparisons require repeated coverage and remain exploratory. No streak punishment or gamification of distress.
- The first lunar-cycle milestone uses 30 days as a rounded visual target. Actual analytical cycles use calculated conjunction timing. Milestones do not promise statistical significance.
- English is the full launch copy. Hindi and Arabic are explicitly preview translations; untranslated UI falls back to English. Locale dictionaries, Intl formatting and RTL are implemented. Native-speaker review is required before claiming full market localization.
- Browser/keyboard dictation is the reliable voice fallback. There is no misleading microphone button or server audio upload.
- Custom CSS supplies the visual system without adding a utility-framework dependency; Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase and Web Crypto follow the preferred stack.
- Weather, precise location, wearables, news, traditional variables, Ask My Patterns and billing are not exposed as fake actions. Their interfaces/schema and staged roadmap preserve extension paths.
- Guest-to-cloud migration and JSON import are deliberately deferred. The sign-in UI explains that spaces are separate. Exports remain useful portable backups, including encrypted notes and their provenance.
