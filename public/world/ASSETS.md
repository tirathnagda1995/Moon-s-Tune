# Fictional World illustrations

`preview-scenes.png` is an original image generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. It is a 2×2 contact sheet: Tokyo side street at dusk; chai beside a Mumbai café window; rainy London café; sunlit Lisbon residential lane. It was copied into this source-controlled directory from the tool's generated-images output. No external photo scraping or competitor assets were used. CSS crops the four quadrants without editing the bitmap.

Generation brief: create four cinematic, editorial photographs of ordinary everyday city moments in one clean, borderless 2×2 grid; quiet Tokyo street at blue hour with warm shop light, chai by a Mumbai window, London rain through café glass, Lisbon laundry and warm street light. Warm natural palette, realistic textures, no identifiable faces, logos, text or watermarks. These are fictional illustrative scenes, not documentary records or current conditions.

The implementation labels city covers “Illustrated city cover” and uses the sample Moment cards only in explicit Product preview mode, with a prominent AI/fictional disclosure and per-card preview labels. The bitmap is not evidence of users, weather, community activity or the appearance of a specific real business.

Rendering/runtime references: [Sharp output and metadata defaults](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/api-output/), [Supabase private buckets](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/buckets/fundamentals), [Supabase downloads](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/serving/downloads).
